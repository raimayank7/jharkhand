How the code BKID0005975 is built

BKID is the bank part: the letters of Bank of India. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 005975 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 825013523 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 825 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 825312), 013 is the bank and 523 is the branch.
